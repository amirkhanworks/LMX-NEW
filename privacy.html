Privacy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Who we are. Luminox Automation ("we") is based in Mumbai, India. This notice explains what personal data we collect through luminoxautomation.com and how we use it.

What we collect.

  • If you join the app waitlist: your email address.
  • If you request a demo: the information you enter in our demo form, for example your name, phone number, area and details about your home. This form is hosted by Typeform.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp, call or email: your contact details and what you tell us.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website.

Why we collect it. To tell you when the Luminox app launches, to arrange and deliver demos and installations, and to reply to your messages. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it for us. Formspree (waitlist submissions), Typeform (demo requests), WhatsApp / Meta (messages you send us), GoDaddy (website hosting) and Cloudflare (website delivery and security). These providers may process data outside India.

How long we keep it. Waitlist emails until the app launches and for up to 12 months after, or until you ask us to delete them. Demo and customer records for as long as needed to provide our service and meet legal obligations.

Your rights. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email info@luminoxautomation.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Grievances. If you have a concern about how we handle your data, contact us at info@luminoxautomation.com.

Changes. We will update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
